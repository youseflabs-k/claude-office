# Office world redesign

The reference concept is [office-world-concept-v1.png](office-world-concept-v1.png).
The built-in Imagegen tool generated the concept and two original texture assets.

Texture prompts:

- `public/textures/limestone-courtyard-v1.png`: seamless square albedo tile of warm pale limestone courtyard paving, top-down view, natural rectangular stones, fine sand-filled joints, cream and greige tones, uniform diffuse light, no objects, text, shadows or perspective.
- `public/textures/pale-oak-v1.png`: seamless square albedo tile of pale warm European oak planks, vertical wide boards, authentic pores and subtle grain, honey cream tones, fine joints and staggered ends, uniform diffuse light, no objects, text, shadows or perspective.

Concept prompt: a full-screen cozyoffice UI over a warm modern Scandinavian creative office pavilion with real tall window openings, an oak-framed glass entrance, sage plaster and exposed timber trim; twelve pale-oak workstations, a lounge and coffee bar; a planted limestone courtyard with olive and birch trees, lavender, ferns, benches, pergola and birdbath; compact translucent navigation and hideable panels.

Eleven GLB assets are generated by `node app/scripts/generate-studio-assets.mjs`,
with source-authored geometry and materials: oak workstation, desk lamp, olive tree,
birch tree, lavender bed, fern planter, river rocks, garden bench, pergola, birdbath,
and outdoor table. Their files live under `public/models/furniture/studio/`.
The live pavilion walls, glazing, door frames and hinged door are authored in
`src/scene/architecture.js`; their openings are actual gaps in the wall geometry.

No third-party garden models or textures were downloaded.

## Grass garden redesign

`app/src/scene/garden.js` authors the garden directly in Three.js: textured lawn,
instanced grass tufts, winding limestone paths, a timber pergola deck, ground-level
shrub and flower borders, and a small reading terrace. Existing local tree,
bench, birdbath, rock, and pergola GLBs are reused. The lawn texture is a seeded
procedural canvas texture; the paths and deck use the previously generated
limestone and oak textures. No additional external assets are required.

Garden seating coordinates remain aligned with the simulation, and planting
beds, the birdbath border, benches, and pergola posts are navigation obstacles.
The lawn is selectable as a floor for walking commands. Every imported office
receives the same garden through the shared environment clone.
